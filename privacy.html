Privacy Policy Last updated: November 2025 This Privacy Policy describes how Play8 Studio (“we”, “our”, “us”) handles user information in the mobile game Carta Arena. We respect your privacy and do not collect, store, or share personal information unless explicitly required for game features such as multiplayer, authentication, or purchases. 📌 1. Information We Collect The app itself does not directly collect personal data. However, third-party services integrated into the game may process limited technical data: a) Photon Multiplayer Used to connect players online. Photon may temporarily process: IP address Device region This is required to route players to the nearest server. We do not store or access this information. b) Firebase Authentication (if enabled) Used only for account creation/login. Firebase may process: Email address Authentication tokens Passwords are never visible to Play8 Studio. We do not share or sell account data. c) Ad Networks (AdMob / Unity Ads) (if enabled) Ads providers may collect: Advertising ID (for ad personalization) Device type Approximate location (non-GPS) You may opt out of personalized ads from your device settings. Play8 Studio does not access this data. d) In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing) Our app offers optional in-app purchases. Google Play may process: Account ID Purchase history Payment details (we DO NOT see any credit card info) Transactions are securely handled by Google Play, not by Play8 Studio. 📌 2. How We Use Information Information processed by third-party services is used only for: multiplayer gameplay account login showing ads processing in-app purchases preventing fraud and improving performance We never sell or share player data. 📌 3. Data Retention We do not store any personal data on our servers. Data managed by third parties follows their own retention policies. 📌 4. Children’s Privacy Our game is intended for general audiences and does not directly collect personal information from children. Ads shown follow Google Play Families policies if applicable. 📌 5.
